User Manual

How to use every part of the STAY BAY HR & Payroll system, step by step. Figures shown in highlighted text are your current settings.

No sections match your search.

1. Getting started

Signing in

  1. Open the website and tap your account (for example Owner or Manager).
  2. Type your password and press Sign in.
  3. After wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked for minutes.

New staff: requesting access

  1. On the sign-in page, tap New staff? Request access.
  2. Enter your name, mobile number, the access you need, a password (twice) and an optional note, then tap Send Request.
  3. The Owner sees the request on the dashboard and in Settings → Users & Roles and approves it (as Manager or Owner) or rejects it. Once approved, your name appears on the sign-in page and you sign in with the password you chose.
Use the hotel's device: requests are saved on the computer or phone where they are made, so register on the device the hotel uses for this system.

Basic and Advanced versions

At the top of the dashboard, slide the circle left for Basic or right for Advanced. Basic is the original layout: Dashboard, Attendance, Salary Payments & Advances, Increments & Incentives, Family & Welfare, Employee Personal Data and Salary & Tax, and salaries are paid in one step with Pay. Advanced adds Leave & Overtime, the payroll approval steps, Reports, Settings and this manual. Both use the same data, and each user's choice is remembered.

First sign-in: change your password straight away from the person icon (top right) → Change my password. The dashboard shows a red reminder until you do.

Signing out

Use the person icon → Sign out / Switch user. If nobody touches the screen for minutes, you are signed out automatically and any unsaved form is closed.

Moving around

  • The red bar of tabs at the top opens each section. A small red number on a tab shows how many items are waiting for action.
  • The Financial Year selector (top right) switches the whole system between years (1 April to 31 March).
  • Most tables can be searched, filtered and sorted (tap a column heading). Long lists are split into pages.
  • Every change is saved straight away. There is no separate Save for the whole page.

2. Who can do what

There are two kinds of account:

  • Owner / Admin: can do everything.
  • Manager: can do everything except approve or reject Advance Salary requests. A Manager creates the request; the Owner decides. To protect that rule, a Manager also cannot edit, settle or remove an approved advance, or create or change Owner accounts.

These checks run inside every action, so a blocked action is refused (and recorded in the audit log) even if someone finds a way to press the button.

3. Dashboard

  • Employees today: total, active, present, absent, on leave, late, on weekly off and on holiday.
  • Payroll: this month's gross salary, deductions, net salary, pending payments and payroll status.
  • Needs Attention: everything waiting for someone: leave requests, advance requests, unfinished payroll months, unmarked attendance, overtime and incentives to approve, missing documents and the backup reminder. Each item has a button that takes you straight there.
  • Charts, upcoming birthdays, work anniversaries and holidays, recent activity and the team list are further down.

4. Employees

Adding an employee

  1. Go to Employees → + Add Employee.
  2. The next Employee ID (for example SBY009) is filled in for you. IDs must be unique.
  3. Fill in the personal, identity, bank and employment details, choose the department, designation and shift, and enter the salary structure.
  4. Press Add Employee. Their profile opens on the Documents tab so you can upload papers.

The form checks formats for you: PAN (ABCPE1234F), Aadhaar (12 digits), IFSC (SBIN0001234), bank account (9–18 digits), mobile (10 digits) and email. It also refuses negative salaries and duplicate IDs.

When someone leaves

Records are never deleted. Open the profile → Change Status and choose Resigned, Terminated or Inactive, with the last working day and a reason. Their history stays available for reports and audits, and they stop appearing in attendance and payroll after their last day.

The employee profile

Click any employee to open their profile. Its tabs are: Overview (all personal, identity, bank and job details), Attendance, Leave (balances and history), Salary (structure and raise history), Payslips, Advances, Incentives, Documents, Tax and Activity (everything the audit log recorded for this person).

Aadhaar, PAN and bank account numbers are partly hidden. Tick Show full numbers to see them; this is recorded in the audit log.

Documents

On the Documents tab, upload Aadhaar, PAN, bank proof, EPF/UAN, ESI, offer and appointment letters, address proof, education and experience papers and a photo (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB each). Required documents are set in Settings → Security & Notifications; anyone missing one appears on the dashboard and in the Missing Documents report.

Daily Attendance Register (with times)

  1. Open Attendance. The register shows today; use the arrows or the date box for another day.
  2. For each employee, check the shift, then enter the check-in and check-out times. The status changes to Present automatically.
  3. Add Permission minutes for an approved short absence, or tick On duty for official work outside the hotel.
  4. Worked hours, late arrival, early leaving and overtime are worked out as you type, including night shifts that end the next morning.
  5. Press Save Register. Overtime of minutes or more is sent to Leave & Overtime for approval.

The register refuses contradictions, for example check-in times for someone marked Absent or on Weekly Off.

Weekly log and monthly calendar (quick marking)

  • Weekly log: click any day to choose a status from a list.
  • Monthly calendar: pick a status (or press P, H, A, O, W or C to clear), then click or drag across days. Click a date to mark everyone for that day; click a name to fill that person's blank days. Right-click (or long-press) a day for all options, including leave. Undo (or Ctrl/⌘ + Z) reverses the last change.
  • Fill unmarked days and Unmarked Sundays → Weekly Off speed up month-end.
A lock symbol beside a name means that month's payroll is approved or paid, so its attendance can't be changed. To correct it, reopen the payroll (see section 8).

6. Leave

Applying for leave

  1. Go to Leave & Overtime → + New Leave Request (or right-click a day in the calendar → Leave).
  2. Choose the employee, leave type and dates, and enter a reason. The box shows how many days it is (holidays are not counted) and the remaining balance.
  3. Tick Approve now to approve at once, or leave it unticked to wait for approval.

Approving, rejecting and cancelling

Pending requests are listed first. Approve writes the leave into attendance automatically. Reject asks for a reason. Cancel removes approved leave and makes those days blank again.

The system stops: leave longer than the remaining balance, overlapping requests, leave on days already marked as worked, and leave in a locked payroll month.

Leave types and balances

Your leave types: . Balances reset each financial year. Loss of Pay leave is unpaid and reduces salary like an absence. Change leave types and yearly days in Settings → Leave Rules.

7. Overtime

  • Overtime from the daily register appears automatically as Pending. You can also add it by hand with + Add Overtime.
  • Pay = hourly wage × hours × rate. The hourly wage is the monthly gross ÷ ÷ the shift's hours. The default rate is .
  • Only Approved overtime is paid, in the payroll of the month it was worked.
  • Overtime can't be recorded on a day the employee is marked absent, on leave or off, or twice for the same day.

8. Payroll and payslips

The five payroll steps

1 · DRAFTFigures update live as attendance, leave, overtime and incentives change.
2 · REVIEWSubmitted for checking. Changes are still allowed.
3 · APPROVEDFigures are frozen and the month is locked.
4 · PAIDEvery employee's payment is recorded.
5 · LOCKEDHappens automatically once everyone is paid.
  1. Open Payroll & Advances and choose the month.
  2. Check the table. Details on any row shows the full breakdown. Warnings tell you about unmarked days or pending items.
  3. Press Submit for Review, then Approve Payroll.
  4. Press Pay All Pending (or Pay on one row), enter the payment date, mode and reference, then Confirm Payment. The same employee can never be paid twice for a month.
  5. When everyone is paid, the month becomes LOCKED.

How salary is calculated

Gross salary − Total deductions = Net salary.

  • Earnings: basic, HRA, conveyance, other allowances, special allowance, approved overtime, holiday and weekly-off work pay, approved incentives and the Diwali bonus.
  • Loss of pay (LOP): one day's pay is the monthly gross ÷ , deducted for each absent day, half day share, Loss of Pay leave day and day before joining or after leaving.
  • PF: of basic from the employee and from the hotel.
  • ESI: from the employee and from the hotel, for staff whose gross is up to .
  • Professional tax: the amount set for each employee (default a month).
  • TDS: the year's income tax spread over the months not yet paid.
  • Advance and loan recovery: each month's instalment, never more than what is still owed.
  • Net salary is rounded to the nearest .

Payslips

Press Payslip (paid months) or Preview (not yet paid) to download a PDF with the hotel name and logo, employee details, paid, leave and LOP days, earnings, deductions, net salary in words, employer PF and ESI and the payment date. Payslips are also on each employee's Payslips tab.

Correcting a paid month

Press Reopen Payroll and enter the reason (required). The payments are cancelled and the month goes back to Draft so you can correct it, then approve and pay again. The original payments and the reason stay in the payroll history and the audit log.

9. Advance Salary and loans

Manager: requesting an advance

  1. In Payroll & Advances, press + Request Advance.
  2. Choose the employee, type (Advance or Loan), amount, monthly recovery, start month and reason, then Send Request.
  3. The request appears at the top of the Salary Advances table as Awaiting Owner approval. You can Withdraw it while it is pending.

Owner: approving or rejecting

  1. The dashboard shows waiting requests. Press Approve on the request row, adjust the amount, recovery or start month if needed, then Approve & Save.
  2. Or press Reject and give a reason; the Manager sees it on the request.

Recovery

  • Each month's instalment is deducted from salary automatically, and stops once the balance reaches zero.
  • Settle records an early lump-sum repayment (Owner only). It can't be more than the outstanding amount.
  • History shows every instalment and settlement with the running balance.

10. Increments and incentives

  • Increments: press + New Increment, choose the employee, effective date, amount and reason. The new salary applies from that month; months already approved or paid stay as they were.
  • Incentives: press + New Incentive, choose the type, month, amount, reason and who recommended it. Tick Approve now, or leave it pending and approve it later from the table. Only approved incentives are paid, in the chosen month's salary.

11. Family and welfare

Record each employee's family members (for insurance, education help and emergencies) and any welfare given: medical help, children's education, marriage gifts, maternity, bereavement, festival gifts, emergency aid and insurance. Each benefit moves from Requested to Approved to Paid.

12. Salary and income tax

  • Choose an employee to see their salary structure, cost to company and the income-tax calculation for the year under the new and old regimes. The cheaper regime is highlighted, and Switch changes it.
  • Enter old-regime claims (rent, 80C investments, health insurance, home-loan interest, NPS) under Salary structure & tax declarations.
  • Download Tax Certificate produces the yearly Form 130 / Form 16 PDF. Fill in the employer TAN, PAN and signatory in Settings → Hotel first.

13. Reports

  1. Open Reports and choose a report from the list.
  2. Set the date, month, employee, department or status filters it offers, and type in the search box to narrow it further.
  3. Click a column heading to sort. Totals are shown at the bottom.
  4. Press Excel (CSV) or PDF to download the whole report (all pages).

Attendance: Daily, Monthly, Employee, Department, Late, Overtime, Leave and Absenteeism. Payroll: Monthly Payroll, Salary Register, Department Payroll, Deductions, Advance Recovery, Incentives and Payments. Employees: Employee Master, Active, Resigned, Department-wise and Missing Documents.

14. Settings

  • Hotel: name, logo, address and the employer details printed on payslips and tax certificates.
  • Departments & Designations: add, rename (employees are updated too) or remove unused ones.
  • Shifts: start and end times and grace minutes. A shift whose end is earlier than its start (like 11 PM – 7 AM) runs past midnight.
  • Holidays: the Tamil Nadu list is pre-filled; add next year's dates when announced.
  • Leave Rules: leave types, days per year and whether they are paid.
  • Payroll Rules: day basis, half-day and unmarked-day rules, weekly offs, holiday and weekly-off work pay, overtime, PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS, rounding, default salary structure and the Diwali bonus. Changes apply to months not yet approved.
  • Users & Roles: add users, reset passwords, disable accounts and view the permissions table.
  • Security & Notifications: sign-out time, lockout rules, which alerts the dashboard shows and which documents are required.

15. Audit log

Every important action is recorded with the date and time, user, role, employee, old and new values and reason. This covers attendance, salary, bank details, leave, overtime, advances, incentives, payroll steps, reopening, settings, users, sign-ins and exports.

Open it from the person icon → Audit log or Settings → Audit Log. Filter by text, module, user or dates, and export to CSV or PDF. Nobody can edit or delete audit entries, including the Owner.

16. Backups and data safety

Important: this website has no central server. All records, documents and the audit log are saved in the browser on the device you use. Another computer or phone will not see them, and clearing the browser's data deletes them.
  1. Go to Settings → Backup & Restore and press Download Backup at least once a week (the dashboard reminds you after 7 days).
  2. Keep the file somewhere safe, for example Google Drive or an email to yourself.
  3. To move to another computer, or to recover, choose the file under Restore from a backup and press Restore Backup. This replaces everything on that device.

If a red bar says the browser cannot save, stop entering data and download a backup, then free up browser storage.

17. Month-end checklist

  1. Make sure every day is marked (Dashboard → Needs Attention shows unmarked days).
  2. Approve or reject all pending leave requests.
  3. Approve or reject pending overtime and incentives.
  4. Owner: decide on any Advance Salary requests.
  5. Open Payroll & Advances for the month, check the figures and Submit for Review.
  6. Check again, then Approve Payroll.
  7. Transfer the salaries, then Pay All Pending with the date and reference. The month locks automatically.
  8. Download payslips and the Salary Register and Payment reports.
  9. Download a backup.

18. Questions and problems

I can't change attendance for a day.
That month's payroll is approved or paid (look for the lock symbol), or the day is approved leave. Reopen the payroll, or cancel the leave in Leave & Overtime.
An employee's pay looks too low.
Press Details on their payroll row. Check the LOP days, then look for absent days, half days or Loss of Pay leave in the calendar.
Overtime or an incentive is missing from the salary.
Only approved items are paid. Approve it in Leave & Overtime or Increments & Incentives before the month is approved.
As Manager, I can't approve an advance.
That's intentional: only the Owner can approve or reject Advance Salary requests.
I forgot my password.
Ask the Owner to set a new one in Settings → Users & Roles → Edit. Only another Owner account can reset an Owner's password and there is no reset email, so create a second Owner account (or keep your password somewhere safe) in case one is forgotten.
My account is locked.
Wait for the lock time shown, then try again with the correct password.
The data is missing on another computer.
Data lives in each browser. Restore your latest backup on that computer.
The site looks like the old version.
Reload without the cache: ⌘ + Shift + R on a Mac, Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows.

19. Glossary

LOP (loss of pay)
Pay deducted for days not worked and not covered by paid leave, weekly offs or holidays.
Paid days
Days in the month minus LOP days.
Comp off
An extra weekly off earned by working on a weekly off or holiday.
PF / EPF
Provident Fund: retirement savings, with contributions from the employee and the hotel.
UAN
Universal Account Number: the employee's 12-digit PF account number.
ESI
Employees' State Insurance: medical cover for lower-paid staff, with contributions from both sides.
PT
Professional tax: a state tax on employment.
TDS
Tax deducted at source: income tax taken from salary each month.
CTC
Cost to company: gross salary plus the hotel's PF, ESI and gratuity costs.
Gross / Net salary
Gross is total earnings before deductions; net is what the employee receives.
Frequently asked questions
How do I mark attendance?

Open Attendance. In the weekly log, click a day and choose the status. In the monthly calendar, choose a status at the top, then click or drag across the days. Changes save straight away.

How is the salary worked out?

From the attendance calendar. Every day is paid except Absent days, half of each Half Day and Loss of Pay leave. Weekly offs, holidays and paid leave are paid. PF, ESI, professional tax, income tax (TDS) and any advance instalment are deducted, and approved incentives are added.

How do I pay the salaries for a month?

Open Salary Payments & Advances (called Payroll & Advances in the Advanced version), choose the month and press Pay All Pending or Pay for one person. Enter the payment date and mode, then confirm. Download each payslip with the Payslip button.

Who can approve an Advance Salary?

Only the Owner. The Manager can request an advance; it shows as "Awaiting Owner approval" until the Owner approves or rejects it.

How does a new staff member get a login?

On the sign-in page they tap New staff? Request access and fill in the form on the hotel's computer. The Owner approves the request from the dashboard or Settings → Users & Roles.

What is the difference between Basic and Advanced?

Basic is the original, simpler layout. Advanced adds leave and overtime, the payroll approval steps, reports, settings and the user manual. Switch with the slider at the top of the dashboard; nothing is lost when you switch.

Where is my data saved? How do I keep it safe?

In the browser on the device you use. Download a backup every week from Settings → Backup & Restore (in the Advanced version, or from the person icon → Settings).

I forgot my password.

Ask the Owner to set a new one in Settings → Users & Roles. If the Owner's password is forgotten, contact the developer below.

The website still looks old after an update.

Reload without the cache: ⌘ + Shift + R on a Mac, or Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows.

Monthly Salary Payments

Pay is worked out from the attendance calendar: every day is paid except Absent (unpaid) days and half of each Half day. Paid offs, govt holidays and comp-off days are paid. Performance incentives are added to the month's gross. Advance recoveries are deducted automatically.
